📊 Sales Forecasting and Business Insights Dashboard

🎯 Project Objective
To contribute to business success by leveraging data analytics techniques, with a focus on time series analysis, to uncover actionable insights and provide accurate sales forecasts.

🧩 Project Overview
This project involved extracting, cleaning, and preparing sales data for analysis using Power BI and DAX. An interactive dashboard was built to visualize trends, highlight key metrics, and enable data-driven decision-making.

✅ Key Deliverables
✅ Data extraction and preprocessing (cleaning, type matching)

✅ Time series analysis for sales forecasting

✅ DAX-based calculations and KPIs

✅ Fully interactive dashboard in Power BI

✅ Insight generation for strategic decision-making

📌 Key Insights
📦 Total Sales Quantity: 22,000 units

💰 Total Sales Value: $1.57 million

📈 Total Profit: $175,260

🌍 Regional Sales: West region contributes ~33% of total sales

👥 Top Segment: 48% of sales from Consumer segment

💳 Payment Mode: 43% of sales via Cash on Delivery (COD)

🗂️ Top-Selling Category: Office Supplies

🚚 Avg. Delivery Time: ~4 days

📚 Learning Outcomes
Through this project, I applied core concepts of data analytics and time series forecasting, honed skills in DAX, and developed a performance-oriented dashboard. This helped in identifying business patterns and supported better strategic planning.
